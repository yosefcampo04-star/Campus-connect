How it works

  1. Message or visit Campus Connect website or Facebook page to check available unit.
  2. Pay the rental fee and sign the borrower's form.
  3. Use the wifi unit for 3 days.
  4. Return the device in good condition.

Payment method: Cash or GCash. Email: connectcampus24@gmail.com

Terms and Conditions / Contract

  • Renter must show school ID.
  • Rental fee must be paid before receiving the device.
  • Unit must be returned on the agreed date and time.
  • Late return has additional charge.
  • Damaged or lost units must be paid by the renter.
